Item 5 — Inversion rule (write-up only)

Add to the spec:

Slide inversion rule. Invert (white text on navy/dark) on:

  • Every cover slide
  • Every section divider
  • Every big-quote slide
  • Every full-bleed-image slide (text overlays photography)

Use light (navy text on white) for:

  • Every standard content slide
  • Three-card, two-column, two-by-two, timeline, layered-list layouts
  • Big-stat slides (amber number reads better on white)

The rhythm of light → dark → light → light → dark across a deck is intentional. It gives the audience visual punctuation between sections without anyone having to think about it.
